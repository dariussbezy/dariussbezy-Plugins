# Better Toasts

Choose how long Discord's pop-up toasts stay, and control how they behave.

Toasts are the small messages that slide in at the bottom ("Copied", "Message sent", errors...). Settings:

- **Duration:** a slider from 2 to 15 seconds. Discord keeps toasts for 5 seconds on its own: shorter values close the toast early, longer ones ask Discord to keep it.
- **Longer messages stay longer:** adds time for each character after the first 40, up to 10 extra seconds.
- **New toast replaces the old one:** the toast on screen is closed the moment another one opens.
- **Vibrate on errors:** a short vibration when an error toast appears.
- **Remember recent toasts:** lists the last 30 toasts in the settings (kept in memory only) so you can read one again or tap it to copy.
- **Show the full toast:** long text uses up to 8 lines instead of being cut short. Off by default, reload Discord after switching.
- **Tap to copy:** tapping a toast copies its whole message. Off by default, reload Discord after switching.
- **Hold to pause:** while a finger is on the toast, its timer stops. Off by default, reload Discord after switching.
- **Swipe to dismiss:** drag a toast away to close it. Off by default, reload Discord after switching.
- **Test toasts:** buttons that show a short and a long toast so you can try the options.

Applies to toasts only, not to the message banners that slide in from the top.
