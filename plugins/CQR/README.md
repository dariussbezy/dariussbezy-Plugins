# Custom Quick Reactions

Choose the quick reaction emojis in the long-press menu, place each one on any spot, and show up to 3 rows.

The settings show a live preview of the long-press menu:

- Tap an empty spot and pick an emoji from Discord's picker: it goes exactly on that spot.
- Tap an emoji, then any other spot, to move it there or swap it with the emoji there. Remove leaves the spot empty.
- Choose 1, 2 or 3 rows. The emoji picker button ends the last row.
- Empty spots show your frequent emojis unless you turn filling off. Reset removes your emojis and turns filling back on.

Works on Android and iOS.
